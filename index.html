Recent Publications

Asymmetric Diamination of In Situ Generated Difluoroenol Species Using Azocarboxamides as Nitrogen Source
Xiongda Xie, Xinke Zhang, Gang Chen, Minghan Yao, Cheng Zhang*, Yue Pan*, Xinfang Xu* • ACS Catalysis • 2025
This study reports a novel asymmetric diamination strategy for difluoroenol species using azocarboxamides as a versatile nitrogen source. The methodology provides efficient access to chiral difluorinated vicinal diamines with high enantioselectivity, offering valuable building blocks for pharmaceutical development.
Practical and Modular Cycloadditions of In-Situ-Formed Exocyclic Vinylcarbenes
C. Zhang,* S. Dong, M. C. Dietl, M. Rudolph, X. Zhang, K. Hong, W. Yi, A. S. K. Hashmi,* X. Xu* • Chem Catalysis • 2025
A practical approach to cycloaddition reactions using in-situ-generated exocyclic vinylcarbenes is described. This modular strategy enables the construction of complex heterocyclic scaffolds with high regio- and stereoselectivity, providing a powerful tool for diversity-oriented synthesis in drug discovery.
