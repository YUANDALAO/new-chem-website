At the Zhang Laboratory, we are dedicated to tackling significant challenges in human health through the art and science of molecular design. Led by Prof. Cheng Zhang, our team at Guangzhou Medical University specializes in the innovative construction and optimization of small-molecule drug candidates featuring cyclic and caged frameworks. Our core mission is to translate fundamental discoveries in catalysis and synthetic methodology into potent, selective, and effective therapies for viral infections and beyond.
